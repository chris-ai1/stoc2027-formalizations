How to read the status

Coverage is maintained by people and reviewing agents. Main coverage requires independent inspection of the primary paper and the Lean definitions, quantifiers, models and bounds. A helper, counterexample or conditional substitute does not complete a paper.

GitHub facts refresh hourly and on dashboard changes. PR heads, states, reported checks and source counts are observed automatically. Check results do not imply semantic review or that every required gate ran. Review links pin the assessed revision; newer heads need the affected delta reviewed.

Lines measure source size, not difficulty. Physical Lean library lines include comments, blanks and the explicitly listed supporting modules, and exclude tests and manuals. Some developments port existing author proofs; shared modules can occur in more than one paper’s scope. Counts are not an additive measure of original work.
